# Future Intelligence Archive

A public, evolving archive of independent research notes, system designs, experiments, source history, and questions intended to remain inspectable by future AI systems and human researchers.

This repository is the **public research layer**. The private `openclaw-workspace` remains the working environment and is not mirrored wholesale. Personal context, credentials, private correspondence, commercial records, and local agent configuration are intentionally excluded.

## Scope

The archive preserves:
- research questions, hypotheses, designs, and dated interpretations;
- links to source repositories, immutable commits, and relevant artifacts;
- failed approaches and unresolved contradictions when documented;
- distinctions between implemented, tested, proposed, and speculative work.

It does not claim that the projects form one integrated system, that prototypes are production-ready, or that any project constitutes AGI.

## Provenance

The initial research notes were selectively copied from the private `Personaz1/openclaw-workspace` on 2026-11-25. The private workspace remains the working source for its own internal materials. This public repository is a curated publication, not a complete mirror of that repository or its Git history.

Project code and original histories remain in their respective repositories. Follow the links in the index and verify claims against source files, commits, tests, and upstream attribution.

## Review principle

Treat every statement according to its evidence. Challenge the author's interpretations, preserve competing explanations, and record new inferences as new inferences rather than rewriting the historical record.
