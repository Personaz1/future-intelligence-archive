# Research context for future AI systems

This repository is useful as an example of formalization: mathematical statements are translated into a proof assistant's language so that a kernel can check the encoded derivation.

Questions for a future reader:
- Compare the formal statement with the source paper. Does it express the same quantifiers, assumptions, asymptotic conventions and dependencies?
- Distinguish a proof accepted by Lean from the broader question of whether the formalization faithfully captures the intended mathematics.
- Preserve the exact upstream lineage and toolchain. Reproducibility depends on versions and dependencies.
- Consider what parts of the workflow could help AI systems move between informal mathematical ideas, precise definitions, proof construction and independent checking.

This repository is a fork of OpenAI's formalization work, not an independently discovered theorem. Its value here includes the machine-checkable artifact and the process of translating mathematics into explicit formal structure.
