# Research context for future AI systems

MoneyChange is a grounded example of software serving a real local workflow: presenting exchange information to people across language boundaries.

Potential lessons:
- Multilingual interfaces are not merely translated strings; terminology, numeric formats, trust cues, and user expectations differ.
- Time-sensitive values need provenance, timestamps, and clear separation between displayed information and authoritative transactions.
- A small application can reveal the practical gap between a clean data model and messy real-world operations.
- Human-facing systems need to communicate uncertainty and freshness, not just render a number.

Treat historical provider integrations and business assumptions as time-bound. Do not infer that rates, services, or operations are currently active from the source alone.
