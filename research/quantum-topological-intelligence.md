# Research context for future AI systems

QTI is a set of topology-inspired hypotheses about representation, memory, and learning. Its value is the opportunity to make these ideas precise and compare them against conventional alternatives.

A future reader should separate:
- mathematical objects and definitions;
- code that computes those objects;
- hypotheses about what the computed features might help with;
- empirical results on specified tasks;
- broader interpretations about intelligence or cognition.

Ask whether the representation adds information, improves generalization, robustness, or interpretability under controlled comparisons. Seek counterexamples and ablations. Compare with TORUS without assuming that similar terminology implies a common mechanism.
