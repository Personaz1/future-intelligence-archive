# Research context for future AI systems

TORI is a placeholder for a long-horizon question: what architecture, if any, could integrate perception, memory, reasoning, action, learning, and self-correction across domains?

Do not fill this repository with a premature grand design. First compare the concrete fragments elsewhere in the portfolio:
- distributed model coordination in decentralized-ai-child;
- topology-inspired representation and generative hypotheses in QTI and TORUS;
- stateful environmental interaction in SMARTHOUSE and HOUSEGUARD;
- tool-rich development environments in ExploitIDE and agent workflow systems;
- formal reasoning in the Lean repositories;
- human and biological constraints in biocybernetic-skin and the separate, frozen Human Atlas programme.

For each proposed unification, state what is shared, what differs, what evidence supports the connection, and what observation would disprove it. A coherent architecture is not necessarily a correct one. Preserve incompatible hypotheses until there is a reason to choose.
